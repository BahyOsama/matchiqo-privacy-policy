This Privacy Policy explains how Matchiqo collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our application. By using Matchiqo, you acknowledge that you have read this policy.

1. About Matchiqo

Matchiqo is a social and dating application that helps users discover and connect with other people, communicate through private conversations, and share text and image posts.

Matchiqo is operated by AppSyntro, referred to in this policy as "we", "us", or "our".

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Matchiqo, we may collect the following information:

  • Account Information: Information needed to create and manage your account, such as your email address and authentication credentials.
  • Profile Information: Information you choose to provide, such as your name, profile details, preferences, and profile pictures.
  • User Content: Text posts, uploaded images, messages, and other content you voluntarily share through the application.
  • Location Information: With your device's permission, Matchiqo may access your device location to support location-based discovery and relevant application features.
  • Support and Safety Information: Information you submit when contacting us, sending feedback, or reporting a user or post.

We do not intentionally collect your device location through GPS when you have not granted the required permission. You can manage location access through your device settings.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for purposes including:

  • Creating, maintaining, and authenticating user accounts.
  • Displaying and managing user profiles.
  • Supporting location-based discovery features.
  • Enabling private messaging and user interactions.
  • Publishing and displaying user-generated posts and images.
  • Processing reports, feedback, and safety concerns.
  • Maintaining application functionality and security.
  • Preventing abuse, fraud, and violations of our policies.
  • Responding to user requests and providing support.

4. Location Permissions

Matchiqo may request access to your device's location to provide location-based features and help you discover relevant users.

  • Location access is subject to your device permission settings.
  • You can deny or revoke location permission at any time through your device settings.
  • Disabling location access may limit features that depend on location.
  • We do not use location permission as authorization to access unrelated information on your device.

The availability and accuracy of location-based features may depend on your device, operating system, and permission settings.

5. Messages, Posts, and User Content

Matchiqo allows users to communicate privately and share posts containing text and images.

Content you choose to publish may be visible to other users according to the application's features and settings. Private conversations are intended for the participants of those conversations.

Please avoid sharing sensitive personal information with other users. You are responsible for the content you choose to upload or share.

Matchiqo provides user safety features, including blocking and reporting functionality where available in the application.

Information submitted through reporting or feedback features may be used to investigate violations, review safety concerns, and take appropriate action under our policies.

We may retain relevant report information when reasonably necessary to investigate abuse, protect users, comply with legal obligations, or enforce our policies.
