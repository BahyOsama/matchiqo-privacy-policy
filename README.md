About Matchiqo

Matchiqo is a social and dating application designed to help people discover connections, communicate through private conversations, and share content with other users.

This repository contains the official public Privacy Policy page for Matchiqo.

Privacy Policy

The Privacy Policy explains how Matchiqo handles user information, including:

- Account and profile information.
- User-generated content, including text posts and images.
- Location information used for location-based features.
- Private conversations and user interactions.
- Data storage, security, and retention.
- User privacy rights and account deletion requests.
- Children's privacy and safety.
